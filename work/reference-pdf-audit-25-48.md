## Audit summary

- 15 exact or publisher-authorized PDFs were opened and inspected: 25, 27, 29, 31–37, 39, 42–44, and 46.
- Entry 26 is demonstrably open access, and an unrestricted full-book PDF is offered by the Library of Congress, but its generated direct-file endpoint was not stable enough to report as a verified durable PDF URL.
- Four cited sources are authoritative living/digital HTML documents without an official consolidated PDF: 30, 38, 40, and 41.
- Four exact works are legitimately access-gated or have no verified lawful open PDF: 28, 45, 47, and 48.
- The most important edition traps are: the 2016-vs-2017 catalog dating of the NASA handbook (31); the 2024 authorized reprint pagination for Salado and Nilchiani (46); and the two different 1992 Schön papers with the same title (48).
