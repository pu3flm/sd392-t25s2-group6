## Accessible surfaces

- The visible history index exposed all pinned items and the 50 most recent non-pinned ChatGPT conversations and Codex tasks.
- The conversation reader exposed paginated, timestamped turns for selected ChatGPT conversations and Codex tasks.
- The local archived-task listing was queried and returned no entries.
- The project catalog was visible and confirmed several ChatGPT project groupings; it did not expose older conversation pages.

## Archive and coverage limits

- The non-pinned history list has no older-page cursor. The oldest relevant item that could be substantiated was a pinned conversation from **18 July 2026**, so this is not a full twelve-month record.
- The available archive listing covers Codex tasks on a host; it did not expose a separate archive of ChatGPT conversations. Consequently, archived ChatGPT history could not be scanned.
- No host or source reported an availability error, but that only confirms that the exposed surfaces responded; it does not establish that the complete account history was available.
- Attached-file contents can be omitted, long prompts can be truncated, and realtime-voice turns can appear duplicated or nested. Only clearly recoverable user intent was retained.
