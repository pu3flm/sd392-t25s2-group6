\section*{Methodological and Authorship Note}

This paper emerged from a longer engineering trajectory of trials and proofs of concept, including a recent voice-mediated design episode involving an engineer and an AI drafting system. That episode is used as a motivating and illustrative design trace, not as an independent empirical corpus and not as proof of the paper's general claims.

The drafting process was collaborative. The engineer supplied the governing problem, technical trajectory, corrections, acceptance criteria, and final judgment. The AI system generated candidate formulations, structural proposals, and literature-linked extensions. During revision, those contributions were jointly integrated into a single engineering argument. Attribution of the drafting process does not convert model-generated interpretation into independent evidence.

Claims in the paper therefore stand or fall according to their stated reasoning, cited external literature, bounded technical results, and disclosed limitations. The design episode may show how a proposed mechanism appears in one trajectory; it cannot establish prevalence, general causal effect, or independent validation of the proposed Context Runtime.
