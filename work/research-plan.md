# Deep-research plan

## Scope and success criteria

- Question: Why do AI-mediated engineering workflows fail when epistemic framing, ontology, intentional relations, observability, authority, and independent verification are not established before implementation?
- Audience: an academic/technical review committee; assumed familiarity with engineering projects but not specialist philosophy.
- Evidence base: authoritative literature plus the 4 September 2026 Context Runtime Design Session as a documentary critical incident.
- Deliverable: self-contained, English, approximately fifteen-page LaTeX article, compiled and visually/structurally verified; dense academic prose with traceable references.
- Boundaries: the session is a mechanism-revealing case, not statistical proof; AI is treated as an amplifier and diagnostic surface, not the sole underlying cause; project-specific terms are labeled as proposals.

## Source classes

1. Original research and canonical primary texts in epistemology-adjacent engineering, ontology, requirements, control theory, distributed systems, testing, V&V, and human automation.
2. Official standards and government/consortium guidance (ISO/IEC/IEEE, NASA, NIST, W3C, OpenTelemetry, RFCs).
3. Peer-reviewed or publicly reviewable empirical work on agentic AI workflows and evaluation.
4. The session transcripts and local artifacts, cited as a private documentary record with explicit evidentiary limits.

## Research sequence

1. **Complete — discovery:** four targeted primary-source lanes covered epistemic/ontological foundations, observability and V&V, AI workflow failure mechanisms, and uncertainty/complexity.
2. **In progress — reconciliation:** packets are merged into the claim ledger with counterevidence and scope limits; bibliographic normalization and final contradiction audit remain.
3. **In progress — synthesis:** `work/report-source.md`, `work/claim-ledger.md`, and `work/case-evidence.md` separate fact, literature, inference, proposal, and documentary observation. The blind Daybreak Erlang report is frozen and hashed; the specification-guided second pass remains pending.
4. **Pending — artifact:** produce the self-contained LaTeX article in `outputs/`, compile twice, inspect page count/text/citations/layout, and correct observed defects.
