# Lawful full-text audit: bibliography entries 1–24

Audit date: 2026-09-05  
Canonical source checked: `outputs/before-the-project.tex`

## Method and result

- All entries 1–24 are external publications; there are no internal project artifacts in this block.
- Title and author identity were checked against a publisher, standards-body, proceedings, author, or institutional-repository record. “Opens: yes” means that the PDF returned as a PDF and its first page/title-author metadata was inspected; page counts below are physical PDF pages unless journal pagination is stated.
- A targeted search found 17 lawful, accessible PDFs that opened. Seven cited editions had no clearly authorized, freely accessible PDF that could be verified: 1, 6, 8, 9, 12, 21, and 22. Those records still have authoritative landing pages and, where applicable, paid/subscription access.
- Copies on Scribd, SciSpace, ResearchGate without a clear author-deposit signal, generic PDF mirrors, and similar aggregators were not recommended. “No accessible PDF located” is a search result, not proof that no authorized manuscript exists anywhere.

## Bibliography corrections surfaced by the audit

1. Add PMLR pages 10835–10866 to `gao2023` if page ranges are being normalized.
2. Consider restoring the publisher’s final question mark in the `gruber1995` title.
3. Replace or supplement the broken HTTPS URL in `gruninger1995` with the official EIL landing page; its linked PDF currently works only over HTTP because the PDF host’s HTTPS certificate does not match the hostname.
4. Keep `giffin2009` as article 081001; ignore `081010` in the MIT filename.
5. Do not silently combine the Kalman DOI’s 491–502 pagination with the manuscript’s congress-book container and 481–492 pagination. Either citation form can be defensible, but the container, pages, and access copy should describe the same presentation.
