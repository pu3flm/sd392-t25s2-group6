# Reference Access and Pinpoint Audit

Companion index for the bibliography of *Before the Project: Epistemic Engineering for Observable, Accountable AI Workflows*.

## Purpose

This index separates four questions that are easy to conflate:

1. Does the cited work and bibliographic record actually match the title and authors named in the manuscript?
2. Is a complete, readable copy available through a publisher, standards body, proceedings site, author, preprint server, or institutional repository?
3. Does the accessible copy use the same edition and pagination as the cited version of record?
4. Does the precise manuscript claim appear on an identifiable page, section, or paragraph of that source?

An accessible preprint can verify substance without preserving the page numbers of the journal version. The audit therefore records **published pagination** and **PDF-object pagination** separately whenever they differ. A direct PDF link means that the file opened and its pages were readable during this audit; it does not imply that every PDF is the version of record.

## Access labels

- **Official PDF:** publisher, standards body, or conference-proceedings file.
- **Author/institutional copy:** full text supplied by an author or repository; pagination may differ from the version of record.
- **Official HTML:** complete authoritative text exists, but no PDF edition was found.
- **Library/edition check:** no reliable open full-text copy was located, or the available copy does not establish the cited edition.
- **Local design trace:** private project material, not an external publication and not searched as one.

## Audit status

The bibliography contains 70 entries. Access checks and claim-level pinpoint checks are being merged here additively; the manuscript is not rewritten from this audit without a separate editorial decision.
